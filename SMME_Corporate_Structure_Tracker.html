Critical Compliance Checklist

Legal and regulatory requirements for South African SMMEs

  • ✓
    CIPC Company Registration Critical
    Cost: R175 | Legal existence and protection
  • ✓
    SARS Tax Number Registration Critical
    Cost: Free | Required for banking and compliance
  • ✓
    Business Bank Account Critical
    Cost: Free (TymeBank, Shoprite Money) | Separate personal/business finances
  • ✓
    B-BBEE Affidavit (EME) High
    Cost: Free (Commissioner of Oaths R50) | Essential for tenders
  • ✓
    SARS Tax Clearance Certificate High
    Cost: Free | Required for government contracts
  • ✓
    UIF Registration (if employing) Medium
    Cost: Free | Compulsory for employers
  • ✓
    Industry-Specific Licenses Medium
    Cost: Variable | COIDA, liquor, health certs, etc.

Important Reminders

  • Keep all registration documents in your digital filing system
  • Set calendar reminders for annual returns (CIPC) and tax submissions
  • B-BBEE affidavit valid for 12 months - set renewal reminder
  • Don't register for VAT until you exceed R1 million turnover

💡 IP Protection Tip

In South Africa, copyright is automatic for work you create, but Trademarks (for your brand) and Patents (for inventions) must be registered with CIPC.

HR & People Management

Employ people correctly from day one

⚖️
Employee vs Independent Contractor
SARS takes this seriously. Misclassification leads to back-pay, penalties & interest.
✅ Likely an Employee
  • Works set hours / schedule
  • Uses your tools & equipment
  • Works at your premises
  • Cannot sub-contract the work
📋 Likely a Contractor
  • Sets own hours
  • Uses own tools
  • Works independently off-site
  • Can sub-contract
