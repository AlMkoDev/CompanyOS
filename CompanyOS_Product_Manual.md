# CompanyOS — Product Overview & Manual

## 1. What is CompanyOS?
**CompanyOS** is a specialized *Virtual Company Operating System (VCOS)* designed to digitally replicate, govern, and optimize the internal workings of a complex organization. 

Unlike traditional ERPs or project management tools that focus on *output*, CompanyOS focuses on **architectural integrity**. It serves as the single source of truth for "How this company works," ensuring that every department, role, and routine is aligned with the organization's core mission.

---

## 2. What it Does
CompanyOS provides a high-fidelity digital twin of a company's operational framework. Its core capabilities include:

### A. Strategic Governance
- **Mandate Management**: Defines the mission, vision, and objectives for every functional unit.
- **KPI Tracking**: Monitors real-time success metrics against predefined targets for every department.
- **Budgetary Oversight**: Tracks Treasury/OpEx allocations and utilization efficiency.

### B. Structural Architecture (The Command Structure)
- **Role Definition**: Breaks down the organization into a hierarchy of Executive, Management, and Individual Contributor roles.
- **Accountability Mapping**: Explicitly documents responsibilities, headcounts, and reporting lines to eliminate ambiguity.

### C. Operational Orchestration
- **Operational Routines**: Documents the daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly, and annual "heartbeat" of the company (e.g., Daily Stand-ups, Monthly Financial Closes).
- **Communication Networks**: Maps how information flows between departments and roles (who talks to whom about what).
- **Infrastructure Data Packs**: Catalogs the essential assets and systems (e.g., CRMs, ERPs, Logs) required to run each unit.

---

## 3. Everyday Usage
CompanyOS is designed to be the "Home Page" for every employee, used differently across the organization:

### For Executives (CEO, CSO, CFO)
- **Strategic Pulse**: Use the dashboard to monitor high-level KPI health and ensure departments aren't drifting from their mandate.
- **Resource Rebalancing**: Review budget utilization and headcount distribution to make informed scaling decisions.

### For Departmental Leadership
- **Operations Bible**: Refer to the *Operational Routines* tab to ensure standard procedures are being followed on schedule.
- **Onboarding & Coaching**: Use the *Command Structure* view to onboard new hires, showing them exactly where they fit and what they are accountable for.
- **Alignment**: Use the *Communication Network* to manage dependencies with other departments (e.g., Sales talking to Marketing about lead quality).

### For Individual Contributors
- **Role Clarity**: Access a clear "Constitution" for their position, listing their core deliverables and responsibilities.
- **Routine Execution**: Follow the *Technical Activities* and *Routines* to understand their daily and weekly priorities, ensuring consistency in execution.
