This specification document is organized into two parts per department:

**Part I — Org Chart:** A visual hierarchy diagram showing every role in the department, their reporting line, headcount, and seniority level. A communication lines table details the key channels, content, and cadence of information flow between roles and with other departments.

### **Department Purpose**

*The Finance Department is the financial nerve centre of Verdant Fields AgriTech Ltd. It owns all money flows — inbound, outbound, and internal — and translates raw financial data into insight that drives strategic decisions. Every other department depends on Finance to budget accurately, pay on time, collect diligently, and report truthfully.*

## **📊  Component 1: Financial Planning & Analysis (FP\&A)**

The forward-looking engine of Finance. Owns budgets, forecasts, scenario models, and the monthly management pack.

**Sub-Sections & Key Activities**

| Section | Detail |
| ----- | ----- |
| Annual Budget Cycle | September–November each year. FP\&A distributes templates, consolidates submissions from all 9 departments, models assumptions, and presents a board-ready budget by 30 November. |
| Rolling 12-Month Forecast | Updated every quarter. Replaces the static annual budget with a live view of expected P\&L, cash flow, and capex. |
| Management Reporting Pack | Distributed within 5 business days of month-end. Contains: P\&L vs. budget, balance sheet, cash flow statement, headcount report, KPI dashboard, and variance commentary. |
| Business Case Support | Any investment \>$10,000 requires an FP\&A-reviewed business case including NPV, IRR, payback period, and sensitivity analysis. |

**Operational Routines & Cadence**

* Daily: Cash position review and short-term cash forecast update

* Weekly: Pipeline and revenue forecast update with Sales

* Monthly: Management pack production and distribution (Day 1–5 post close)

* Quarterly: Rolling forecast refresh and board presentation

* Annually: Full budget cycle (Sep–Nov), 3-year strategic plan update

**Data Pack — Systems & Data Assets Required**

| \# | Data Asset / System |
| ----- | ----- |
| 1 | Chart of Accounts (ERP) |
| 2 | Budget templates (Excel/Anaplan) |
| 3 | Actuals feed from GL |
| 4 | Headcount data from HRIS |
| 5 | Revenue pipeline from CRM |
| 6 | Prior-period comparatives |
| 7 | Board-approved budget baseline |

## **📒  Component 2: Accounting & General Ledger**

Owns the books of record. Responsible for all journal entries, reconciliations, and the integrity of the general ledger.

**Sub-Sections & Key Activities**

| Section | Detail |
| ----- | ----- |
| Month-End Close | Target: 5 business days. Sequence: sub-ledger close → accruals → prepayments → depreciation → intercompany → TB review → sign-off. |
| Balance Sheet Reconciliation | Every balance sheet account reconciled monthly with supporting documentation uploaded to the document management system. |
| Fixed Assets Register | All assets \>$500 capitalized, depreciated using straight-line method, physically verified annually. |
| Intercompany Accounting | All intercompany transactions matched and eliminated monthly before consolidated reporting. |

**Operational Routines & Cadence**

* Daily: Post authorized journal entries, review overnight bank transactions

* Weekly: AP/AR sub-ledger reconciliation review

* Monthly: Full close process — Day 1–5, BS reconciliations Day 3–6, sign-off Day 5

* Quarterly: Fixed asset physical count and depreciation review

* Annually: External audit preparation, year-end close, financial statements

**Data Pack — Systems & Data Assets Required**

| \# | Data Asset / System |
| ----- | ----- |
| 1 | General Ledger (ERP — SAP/NetSuite) |
| 2 | Sub-ledger feeds (AP, AR, Payroll) |

## **📤  Component 3: Accounts Payable (AP)**

Controls all outgoing payments to vendors and suppliers. Ensures invoices are legitimate, approved, and paid on time.

**Sub-Sections & Key Activities**

| Section | Detail |
| ----- | ----- |
| Invoice Processing | 3-way match: PO → goods receipt → invoice. Invoices without a PO require retrospective approval from budget holder. |
| Payment Runs | Weekly payment runs every Thursday. Emergency payments require CFO approval and processed same-day. |
| Vendor Master Management | New vendors onboarded with full due diligence: tax ID, banking details, compliance check. Changes require dual authorization. |
| AP Reconciliation | AP aging reviewed weekly; any invoice \>30 days past due escalated to Finance Manager. |

**Operational Routines & Cadence**

* Daily: Invoice receipt, validation, and PO matching

* Weekly: Payment run (Thursday), aging review, vendor query resolution

* Monthly: AP sub-ledger close, vendor statement reconciliations

* Quarterly: Vendor master data audit — remove inactive/duplicate vendors

## **📥  Component 4: Accounts Receivable (AR)**

Manages all money owed to the company. Generates invoices, collects payments, and protects cash flow.

**Sub-Sections & Key Activities**

| Section | Detail |
| ----- | ----- |
| Invoicing | Invoices raised within 24 hours of goods/service delivery. Electronic invoicing preferred. All invoices include payment terms (Net 30 default). |
| Collections | Day 15: first reminder. Day 25: second reminder. Day 35: formal notice. Day 45+: escalate to Finance Manager and legal hold. |
| Credit Control | New customers assessed for creditworthiness before credit terms granted. Credit limits set by Finance Manager. |
| AR Reconciliation | Customer statements reconciled monthly. Any unapplied cash investigated within 48 hours. |

**Operational Routines & Cadence**

* Daily: Apply customer payments, review unapplied cash

* Weekly: AR aging review, collections follow-ups

* Monthly: AR close, customer statement reconciliations, bad debt provision review

* Quarterly: Credit limit review for top 20 customers

## **🏦  Component 5: Treasury & Cash Management**

Manages the company's liquidity position, banking relationships, and foreign exchange risk.

**Sub-Sections & Key Activities**

| Section | Detail |
| ----- | ----- |
| Cash Flow Forecasting | 13-week rolling cash flow forecast updated weekly. Reviewed by CFO every Monday. |
| Bank Account Management | All bank accounts authorized by CFO. Dual-signatory requirement for transactions \>$25,000. |
